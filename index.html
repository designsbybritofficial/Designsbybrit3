BEFORE YOU BOOK

Good to
know.

How long is the turnaround time?

Most projects have a turnaround time of up to one week. Complex projects may require additional time.

Is a deposit required?

Yes. A deposit is required before design work begins. The remaining balance is due upon completion.

How many revisions are included?

One revision is included with each project. Additional revisions may require an extra fee.

How will I receive my design?

Your completed design will be delivered digitally as high-resolution files.

Do you offer printing?

DesignsByBrit currently provides digital design services. Print-ready files can be supplied when needed.
